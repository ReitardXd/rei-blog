Deleted my old posts. If you saw my X earlier this year, you’d have some context, but the short version is: I wasn’t happy with what I was writing.

[Twitter post](https://x.com/RiderHog1970/status/2083994178414170196?s=20). Yeah, I know it has two likes, but that’s besides the point.

I wasn’t really happy with what I was writing because it was low-hanging fruit. Things like solving Hack The Box machines and solving simple Codeforces problems are objectively low-hanging fruit because they’re easy to do and require less technical depth. Granted, I was solving some pretty difficult problems on Codeforces compared to the average Joe, but that’s besides the point.

Granted, in the age of LLMs, this stuff has a pretty low ceiling. You can literally just ask an agent to pop an HTB box. I wanted to write things that couldn’t be generated.

My main motive right now is to try difficult things, fail at them, and write about them. Maybe, just maybe, I might get lucky and write a good post where I manage to tackle a problem and come up with a good solution. Who knows? :)

So expect the next blog posts to be of higher quality (hopefully).
